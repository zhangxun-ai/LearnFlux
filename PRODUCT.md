# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The current primary user is the project owner, using LearnFlux on a personal Mac to study and organize learning materials.

## Product Purpose

LearnFlux is a self-hosted AI learning workbench. It brings videos, audio, documents, and text into a personal learning flow: import material, create transcripts or parsed text, use AI to explain and organize it, continue studying, and keep useful notes in the user's own knowledge system.

## Positioning

The product connects source material with reviewable learning notes in one personal workflow. Its current center is the user's own learning practice, not a hosted multi-tenant service.

## Operating Context

- The project is currently used locally by its owner on macOS.
- Local transcription and other local services may be part of the owner's environment; external AI and content APIs are configured separately.
- Obsidian is an available destination for notes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The web application supports importing links, local media, documents, and text.
- Existing product areas include single-item and series learning, transcript reading and playback, review, history, and Obsidian synchronization.
- The existing workbench and its routes are functional product surfaces. Visual changes must preserve their behavior.
- The project is not currently launched as a public website or hosted service.
- The future release and business model is undecided: full source release and a paid product remain open options.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: LearnFlux.
- The user selected a fresh, light nature illustration direction for the local landing page and approved matching workbench screens.
- Workbench visual changes are additive and must preserve real routes, controls, and data behavior.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains the working web application and existing product assets.

## Product Principles

- Keep the learning path centered on source material, understanding, review, and note keeping.
- Preserve the user's local workflow and control over configured services.
- Describe only capabilities that are present in the repository.
- Keep future distribution and business choices open until the user decides.
